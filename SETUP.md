
# Mr. Montimurro: Alert Setup Instructions

## 1. Get Your Free Notification URL
The app is built to use **Formspree**—it's free and takes 30 seconds.
1. Go to [Formspree.io](https://formspree.io/) and create an account.
2. Create a "New Form" and name it "Tutoring Alerts".
3. In the "Settings" tab, copy your **Endpoint URL** (it looks like `https://formspree.io/f/xyzkjlp`).

## 2. Connect Your Phone (1-203-400-2491)
1. In your Formspree dashboard, set the **Target Email** to your carrier's text gateway to get an SMS:
   - For **Verizon**: `2034002491@vtext.com`
   - For **AT&T**: `2034002491@txt.att.net`
   - For **T-Mobile**: `2034002491@tmomail.net`
2. This way, every time someone books, you get an **instant text message**.

## 3. Activate the App
1. Open your live tutoring website.
2. Go to the **"Book a Session"** page.
3. Click the **©** (Copyright) symbol in the footer at the very bottom.
4. A private **"Admin Setup"** window will appear.
5. Paste your Formspree URL and click **"Save & Enable"**.

**Your alert system is now live!** You can test it by making a fake booking. You should get a text message and an email immediately. 

*Privacy Note: Your phone number and the Formspree URL are stored only in your browser/app logic and are never shown to the students.*
